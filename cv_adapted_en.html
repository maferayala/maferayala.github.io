Career Summary

I am an organized and proactive professional committed to humanitarian action, with over eight years of experience in information management within humanitarian organizations, supporting vulnerable populations including migrants, people in need of international protection, people living in extreme poverty, and internally displaced populations. My background in geographical analysis strengthens my ability to understand territorial patterns, identify trends, and support operational planning and evidence-based decision-making. I have experience designing and implementing national surveys and data collection exercises across the full data cycle, from form design and field collection to processing, analysis, database management, analytical processes, KPI monitoring, and the development of high quality data products, dashboards, interactive reports, and evidence-based action plans. I specialize in the responsible management and protection of sensitive information, supporting monitoring, evaluation, and learning throughout the humanitarian project cycle. I have experience communicating technical findings through reports and scientific publications, providing data and analysis to support programme implementation, reporting, and fundraising processes, while contributing to strengthening local capacities through technical knowledge transfer and responsible data practices.
