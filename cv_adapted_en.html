Career Summary

I am an organized, proactive professional committed to humanitarian action. I have over seven years of experience in information management and systematization for humanitarian organizations, working with vulnerable populations, including people in need of international protection. Thanks to my specialized academic background in geographical analysis, I possess a strong capacity for territorial analysis, which allows me to identify trends and support operational planning in the field. I have experience in designing data collection tools, managing databases, protecting sensitive information, data analysis, and developing dynamic dashboards that have directly contributed to monitoring, evaluation, and learning processes, supporting evidence-based decision-making in all phases of the humanitarian project cycle. Additionally, I have experience in preparing and communicating technical results, demonstrated through my published scientific articles, which reflects my ability to present findings clearly and rigorously. I have coordinated information collection teams in complex contexts, contributing to strengthening local capacities through technical knowledge transfer and ensuring compliance with international data protection standards.
